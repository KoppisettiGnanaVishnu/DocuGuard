College Attendance Policy

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% to be eligible to attend the final examinations.

Students with attendance below 75% may not be permitted to write the final examinations.

The college library is open from 8 AM to 8 PM on weekdays.

Students must carry their college identity card while entering the library.

The examination fee must be paid before the announced deadline.

Students can access their examination hall ticket after completing the required fee payment.
